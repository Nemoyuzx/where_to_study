## 常见问题 / Common questions

### 没有教务账号可以体验吗？ / Can I try the app without a school account?

在设置的“关于本应用”中选择“浏览内置示例数据”。示例内容为虚构数据，供体验课程、空教室和教学日历使用；返回真实数据后才可使用自己的教务账号。

Choose **Browse Built-in Sample Data** in Settings → About This App. The fictional schedule, classrooms, and calendar can be explored without a school account. Return to live data to use your own academic-system account.

### 课表或空教室刷新失败怎么办？ / Why will schedules or classrooms not refresh?

当前教务连接仅适用于北京邮电大学，并不支持任意学校账号。先确认能够在学校教务服务正常登录，再检查网络和设置中的学期信息。空教室只查询当天；失败时可查看先前保存的课表，稍后重试。学校服务的可用性和原始数据由学校管理，应用维护者不能重置学校账号或修改学校数据。

The current academic integration supports BUPT accounts only. Check whether you can sign in to the university's academic service, then check your network and term settings. Classroom queries cover today only. Previously saved schedules remain available offline. The maintainer cannot reset university accounts or change university records.

### 班车或活动信息不准确怎么办？ / What if shuttle or event information is inaccurate?

请查看条目的来源和更新时间，并以原始通知为准。可以通过邮件提供公开条目的标题、日期和来源链接，帮助维护者排查解析问题。

Check the source and update time, and verify against the original notice. Email the public item's title, date, and source link to report a parsing issue.

### 如何移除保存在本机的数据？ / How do I remove locally stored data?

在设置的“本地数据”中选择“清除本地数据”，并阅读确认提示。该操作会清除应用保存的凭据、缓存、收藏、偏好和应用管理的提醒，不会删除学校保存的记录。

Use **Settings → Local Data → Clear local data** and read the confirmation. This removes app-managed credentials, caches, favorites, preferences, and reminders. It does not delete records held by the university.
